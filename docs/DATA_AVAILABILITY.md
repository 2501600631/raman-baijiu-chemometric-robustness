# Data availability

The raw experimental data underlying this study are **not distributed through this public GitHub repository**. This includes the 80 × 994 Raman spectral matrix, the 80 × 8 GC-MS reference-concentration matrix, and any sample-level experimental metadata that could be subject to confidentiality or data-ownership restrictions.

The public repository contains non-sensitive methodological and provenance materials, including the analyte-specific inclusion/exclusion manifest, analysis code, workflow specification, and software-environment information.

Access to the underlying experimental data may be requested from the **corresponding author** on reasonable request, subject to permission from the data owner and applicable confidentiality restrictions. Requests should be directed to the corresponding author identified in the manuscript/publication.

Large derived archives, including the complete 806,400 split-specific result records and full CRS intermediate/result tables, are retained by the authors but are not included in the public GitHub repository. Where access is required for scientific verification, availability should be discussed with the corresponding author.
